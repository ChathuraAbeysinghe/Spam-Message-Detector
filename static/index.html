🎯 Why We Prioritize High Precision (98.28%)

In SMS communications, a False Positive (classifying an important banking or family SMS as spam) is far more disruptive to users than a False Negative (a spam ad slipping in). Our Multinomial Naive Bayes pipeline with Laplace smoothing (α=0.1) achieves exceptional precision to ensure legitimate messages are never wrongfully blocked.

Architecture Details
  • Classifier: Multinomial Naive Bayes (α=0.1)
  • Feature Extraction: TF-IDF (1-2 N-Grams, 5,000 max features)
  • Dataset: UCI SMS Spam Collection (5,158 clean rows)
  • Train / Test Split: 80% Train / 20% Stratified Test
  • Database Storage: SQLite 3 with indexed timestamps
  • Backend API: FastAPI 0.100+ (Asynchronous Python)
